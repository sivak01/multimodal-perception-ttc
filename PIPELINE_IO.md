## Notes

- **Tuple vs. dict track formats**: LiDAR/radar tracks (Steps 3.1/3.2) are lists of `(sample_id, timestamp, [x,y,z])` tuples; camera tracks (Steps 3.3/3.4) are dicts with a `trajectory` list of point dicts. Step 4 and Step 5 both handle these two formats explicitly — see `load_lidar_or_radar_tracks()`/`load_camera_tracks()` in Step 4 and `load_single_sensor_tracks()`/`load_camera_tracks_df()` in Step 5.
- **`camera` vs. `camera_mono`**: `camera` (2.3.1 → 3.3) borrows depth from LiDAR (median depth of LiDAR points inside each YOLO box) and is the more accurate of the two camera baselines. `camera_mono` (2.3.2 → 3.4) is genuinely LiDAR-free, using similar-triangles depth-from-height, and exists specifically to isolate how much of `camera`'s accuracy comes from borrowed LiDAR depth vs. the camera detections themselves.
- **`camera_mono` is not part of Step 4's fusion** — it's a standalone comparison baseline in Steps 5/6 only, not merged into `fused`.
- **Non-idempotency**: Steps 3.1, 3.2, 3.3, 3.4, and 4 each `shutil.rmtree()` their own output directory at the start of their setup cell, so re-running any of them produces a clean single-run result without manual cleanup.

See `CLAUDE.md` for the full architecture notes, known fixes and their measured effect, and the track-fragmentation investigation.
